## Why this experiment could run at all

Every prior attempt to obtain rendered UI evidence had failed (GAP-001): the
`aosp_atd` image under `-accel off` produced **black** screencaps, and the
`google_apis` image under software emulation **system-ANRed** before the app
launched. Enabling the KVM device node for the runner user removed both
failures. The whole capture now takes **2 m 45 s**.

---

## Rendering verification

The central risk was mistaking a black frame for a working screen. Every
captured frame was measured:

| Frame | Size | Unique colours | Mean luma | Non-black ratio | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `00-home.png` | 1080×2400 | 28 708 | 85.30 | 1.0000 | RENDERED |
| `01-login.png` | 1080×2400 | 1 009 | 28.55 | 0.9739 | RENDERED |
| `02-register.png` | 1080×2400 | 814 | 28.52 | 0.9738 | RENDERED |
| `03-backgrounded.png` | 1080×2400 | 30 180 | 84.33 | 1.0000 | RENDERED |
| `04-relaunch.png` | 1080×2400 | 1 009 | 28.55 | 0.9739 | RENDERED |
| `final-screen.png` | 1080×2400 | 1 009 | 28.55 | 0.9739 | RENDERED |

The low mean luma on app frames is the app's intentional dark theme, not an
unrendered buffer — confirmed by the colour counts and by direct visual review
below. The Android launcher frames (`00`, `03`) sit at ~85 mean luma with ~30 k
colours, proving the emulator's rendering pipeline is healthy independently of
the app.

`04-relaunch.png` and `final-screen.png` are byte-identical
(`sha256:32b85059e1933e61…`), as expected — both capture the same post-relaunch
state.

## UI-01 — Login surface — PASS

Visually reviewed (`01-login.png`). The rendered screen shows:

- Title **"Welcome Back"**, subtitle "Sign in to view your highlights"
- Email and Password fields with visible placeholder text
- Primary **Sign In** button, high-contrast cyan on dark
- Secondary **Create Account** text action

Layout inspection: no clipping, no overlapping elements, no truncated text. The
status bar (clock, wifi, signal, battery) and the gesture-nav pill are clear of
content — safe areas are respected at 1080×2400/420 dpi. Vertical rhythm is even
and the content block is centred. Both fields and both controls are visible and
enabled in this state.

## UI-02 — Navigation via a real rendered control — PASS

The "Create Account" node was located in the live `uiautomator` dump and tapped
at its true centre, **(540, 1702)** — derived from the rendered bounds, not
hardcoded. The app navigated to the registration surface (`02-register.png`):

- Title **"Create Account"**
- Email and **"Password (min 6 chars)"** fields — the constraint is surfaced to
  the user before submission
- Primary **Continue** button
- Secondary **"Already have an account?"** back-action
- A two-step progress indicator at the top, first segment active

No clipping, overlap or truncation. This confirms the control is genuinely
hit-testable at its rendered position, which a black frame could never have
established.

## UI-03 / APP-02 — Background, kill, relaunch, state restoration — PASS

Sequence executed: `KEYCODE_HOME` → capture → `am force-stop com.sportreel.app`
→ relaunch.

```
Starting: Intent { cmp=com.sportreel.app/.MainActivity }
Status: ok
LaunchState: COLD
TotalTime: 2311
WaitTime: 2317
```

After a forced kill the app relaunches cleanly (**2 311 ms**) and returns to the
**login** screen, not the half-completed registration screen it was on when
backgrounded. For an unauthenticated session that is the correct behaviour: no
stale partial-signup state is restored, and no crash occurs on the kill/restart
path. `03-backgrounded.png` confirms the app left the foreground properly rather
than leaving a stuck surface.

## What these results do NOT establish

- Only the **unauthenticated** surface was exercised. Sign-in was not performed,
  so no operator screen (upload, pipeline, review, reels, approval) has been
  seen. Those screens remain entirely unvalidated.
- No error states, loading states or empty states were provoked.
- Only one device profile and one OS version (Android 15 / API 35). DEV-02
  multi-version coverage is still NOT RUN.
- Emulator only. Per the brief this is explicitly not transferable to physical
  device readiness (BLOCKER-005).
